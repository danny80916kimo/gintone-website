04 · THE CALM

The world suddenly goes quiet.

22/36 · GT400

Honestly, part of this app was built for myself. The more choices I have, the louder my head gets. When a roll of film makes most of the decisions for me in advance, the world suddenly goes quiet the moment I frame a shot.

With less to think about, there is less to worry about. Little by little, I found calm in photography.
